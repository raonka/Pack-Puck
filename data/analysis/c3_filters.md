- The raw path reproduces the post-discard tables' median and IQR at every marker (asserted by the script).
- Ground truth: ±0.5 m at 300 m (Methodology §5.3); cone-position error is common to every filter and both bandwidths.

## Order-statistic check for max-of-3

Predicted shift = 0.846 × σ, where σ = sample standard deviation of the post-discard raw readings at that marker. Observed shift = max-of-3 median − raw median. σ is used for this check only; it is not a reported statistic (the paper reports median and IQR).
