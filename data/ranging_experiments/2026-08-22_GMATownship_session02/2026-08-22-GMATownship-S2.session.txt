D1  ATTEMPTS BELOW SPEC, 406.25 STATIC. N=100 per marker is the methodology 11
    requirement. Achieved on the kept 406.25 static files: 100, 90, 90, 90, 110,
    100, 100, 90, 90, 90 at 30..300 m -- six of ten markers at N=90 (shortfall 10).
    Recorded as a DEVIATION against the sessions-per-tier requirement, not as a discard, per the
    day-2 QC instruction. The 1625 static run met or exceeded N=100 at every
    marker (100,100,110,110,110,110,110,110,110,130). Root cause is the dwell
    clock: 90 attempts x 686 ms = 61.7 s, i.e. a ~60 s dwell was run where 68.6 s
    is needed for 100 attempts at the newly measured 406.25 kHz cadence. This is
    the deviation the day-3 dwell change is meant to remove.

D2  ARRIVAL LOG PRESENT BUT NOT FILED WITH THE SESSION. The methodology 7.3 operator
    arrival log EXISTS for both stop-and-go runs -- phone-stopwatch lap sheets, kept
    as stopwatch_laps_data_406.csv and stopwatch_laps_data_1625.csv. This is the
    first outing to satisfy 7.3, and dwell-to-marker attribution for both stop-and-go
    runs is OPERATOR-CONFIRMED, not heuristic.

    The deviation is procedural, not evidentiary:
      (a) The lap files were left in tools/offload/offload/, which is GITIGNORED, and
          were not filed with the session or transcribed into MARKER_ARRIVAL_LOG as
          methodology 7.4 requires. Copies are now filed alongside this log.
      (b) STOPWATCH_START_UTC was not recorded to the second (methodology 7.3 step 1).
          Alignment had to be recovered from the data instead of read from the log --
          see MARKER_ARRIVAL_LOG below. It recovered cleanly, but that is luck, not
          procedure: the conversion in methodology 7.3 needs STOPWATCH_START_UTC and
          BOOT_UTC, and neither was written down.
      (c) The lap convention used (press at the END of each dwell and each transit)
          is not the one methodology 7.3 step 2 specifies (one press ON ARRIVAL at
          each cone). Both are recoverable, but the protocol should state which.

    Nothing about the numbers is in doubt; the filing and the two missing fields are.

D7  TOOL HEURISTIC DISAGREES WITH THE ARRIVAL LOG, 406.25 STOP-AND-GO.
    qc_session.py nearest-marker matching returned no dwell for the 180 m and 240 m
    markers, matched two adjacent segments (211-259 s, 261-283 s -- one dwell split
    in two) both to 270 m, and matched the 409-486 s segment to 210 m. The operator
    arrival log resolves all of it: ten dwells, single monotonic 300->30 descent.
    The QC tables recompute the stop-and-go rows on lap windows and supersede the
    tool's stop-and-go output. The 1625 run needed no correction (tool and lap log
    agree at all ten markers). Cause: the 406.25 offset is comparable to half the
    30 m marker spacing, so nearest-marker matching on the reading shifts
    assignments.

    IMPORTANT: the mis-assignment moved which MARKER a dwell was labelled with, but
    barely moved the statistics -- recomputing on lap windows changes no 406.25
    median by more than 0.6 m and no 1625 median by more than 0.3 m. The per-marker
    medians reported before and after the correction agree; what changed is that
    two markers now have rows at all.

    ACTION for the tool (not done here): qc_session.py should accept the arrival log
    as an input and use it for dwell attribution, falling back to the heuristic only
    when absent. Today the heuristic is the only path even when ground truth exists.
